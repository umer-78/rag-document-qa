# Expenses and Travel

## Principle

Spend the company's money as carefully as your own, and never spend it on
something you would be uncomfortable explaining in an all-hands.

## Limits without pre-approval

Up to PKR 25,000 per item, or PKR 60,000 per month in total, no pre-approval is
needed. Above that, ask your manager in writing first.

## Claiming

Claims go through the expense portal within 30 days, with a photo of the
receipt. Reimbursement lands with the next payroll run if the claim is filed
before the 25th of the month.

## Travel

Economy class for flights under 6 hours, premium economy above. Hotels up to
PKR 30,000 a night in Pakistan and USD 180 a night elsewhere. Book at least 14
days ahead when the trip is planned. Airport transfers and visa fees are covered.

## Daily allowance

A flat PKR 5,000 per day inside Pakistan and USD 60 per day abroad for meals and
incidentals. No receipts needed for the daily allowance; it is not paid when
meals are provided by the event.

## Client entertainment

Client meals are reimbursed up to PKR 8,000 per head, with the names of everyone
present recorded in the claim. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Home internet and phone

PKR 6,000 a month towards internet, paid automatically with salary. A phone bill
is reimbursed only for people carrying an on-call phone.

## What is not covered

Fines, traffic tickets, personal travel extensions, airport lounge memberships,
and upgrades booked on the day of travel.
