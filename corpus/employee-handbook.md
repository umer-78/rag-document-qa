# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

## Working hours

Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 Pakistan Standard Time. Outside core hours you
choose when you work, as long as your week adds up to 40 hours. Anything past
45 hours in a week should be flagged to your manager: consistently long weeks
are treated as a planning problem, not as commitment.

## Remote and office work

Everyone is remote by default. The Lahore office is open Monday to Thursday and
has 24 desks, booked through the internal desk app. Teams meet in person at
least once a quarter; the company pays travel and one night of accommodation for
those visits.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees get 24 days of paid annual leave a year, plus public
holidays. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per completed month. Up to 5 unused
days carry into the next year and must be used before 31 March; anything above
5 days is paid out at the end of December.

Leave is requested in the HR portal at least 7 days ahead for absences of 3 days
or more. Your manager answers within 2 working days. Requests are declined only
when two people on the same team are already away.

## Sick leave

10 paid sick days a year, no medical certificate needed for the first 3
consecutive days. Sick days do not come out of annual leave.

## Parental leave

Primary carers get 16 weeks at full pay, secondary carers 4 weeks at full pay.
Both can be taken in up to three blocks within the first 18 months.

## Probation and notice

Probation is 3 months, extended once at most. Notice is 30 days during the first
year and 60 days after that, on both sides.

## Equipment

Every employee gets a laptop, an external monitor and a PKR 60,000 budget for a
desk, chair and accessories, refreshed every 3 years. Equipment stays with the
company and is returned on the last working day.

## Learning budget

PKR 120,000 per person per year for courses, books and conferences, approved by
your manager. Certification exam fees are covered twice per certification.
